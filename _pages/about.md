Hi, I'm **Guoan Xu**. I am currently a Ph.D. researcher at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), Australia, under the supervision of **[Prof. Wenjing Jia](https://profiles.uts.edu.au/Wenjing.Jia)**. Before pursuing my Ph.D., I received my Master's degree from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT) in 2022, supervised by **[Prof. Guangwei Gao](https://guangweigao.github.io/)**.

My research interests lie in **Computer Vision** and **Deep Learning**, with a particular focus on **Visual Understanding** and **Visual Generation**. 

I am always open to academic discussions and research collaborations. Feel free to reach out!
